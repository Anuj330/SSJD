About our society

A cooperative that puts members first

Shree Shyam Jan Kalyan is a member-owned urban thrift & credit society serving families, farmers and small businesses across Maharashtra. Every rupee you deposit is reinvested into the community — funding homes, harvests and enterprises.

No shareholders to please. Just transparent rates, conservative lending and people who know your name.
